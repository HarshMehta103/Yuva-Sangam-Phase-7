    About the EBSB Club and our journey

    Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat (EBSB) is a Government of India initiative that pairs states and union territories to build deeper cultural, social and academic ties among the people of India. Under this umbrella, Yuva Sangam brings student delegations from paired states together for a shared, immersive experience, anchored in five themes: Parampara (tradition), Paryatan (tourism), Paraspar Sampark (people-to-people connect), Prodyogiki (technology) and Pragati (progress).

    The IIT Jodhpur EBSB Club has been the institute’s home for this exchange since its early phases, coordinating logistics, cultural programming and campus engagement for visiting delegations. Through this platform, we strive to celebrate India’s diversity, foster mutual understanding and create lasting friendships between the youth of our paired states.

    Itinerary for the Sikkim delegation, hosted by IIT Jodhpur

    RangiloRajasthan

    Most people picture a desert. Rajasthan is that, and a great deal more: tigers and leopards, lakes and a hill station, forts on every ridge, temples carved a thousand years ago, and a real part of India’s science story.

    Every step, an adventure.Every breath, something new.Every glance, a window into history.

    More than a desert

    Rangilo: a city for every colour

    Rangilo means colourful, and Rajasthan names its cities that way. The delegation stays in one that has two of these names.

    Pink CityJaipur

    Painted terracotta pink in 1876 to welcome the Prince of Wales, and kept that way.

    You’re hereBlue CityJodhpur

    Indigo-washed houses crowd the old town below Mehrangarh.

    You’re hereSun CityJodhpur

    Bright, clear sunshine for most of the year.

    Golden CityJaisalmer

    A yellow sandstone fort that glows at sunset, deep in the Thar.

    City of LakesUdaipur

    Palaces rise straight out of Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar.

    The week, through a jharokha

    A jharokha is the carved window of a Rajasthani palace. As you read the schedule, the window beside it opens onto the Rajasthan behind each stop. Timings are tentative and may shift closer to the visit.
